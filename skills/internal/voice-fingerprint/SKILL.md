# Voice Fingerprint

Use approved text only. Require at least three samples and 300 words; otherwise report insufficient evidence. Persist schema version, profile id, corpus digest, sample/word counts, and measurable features. Report per-feature deviation, never a human/AI verdict. Writing samples outrank generic style rules unless meaning, evidence, safety, or the brief conflicts. Do not infer biography or fabricate voice from one example.
