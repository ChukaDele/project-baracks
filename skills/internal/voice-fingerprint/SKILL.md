# Voice Fingerprint

Use approved text only. Require at least three samples and 300 words; otherwise report insufficient evidence.

Persist the profile id, corpus digest, sample and word counts, measurable features, characteristic vocabulary, repeated phrases, and a small set of representative approved snippets. The snippets exist so a drafting agent can see real language, not only statistics.

Writing samples outrank generic style rules unless meaning, evidence, safety, or the brief conflicts. Use snippets as evidence of cadence, diction, and sentence construction. Do not mechanically copy them into unrelated contexts.

Report per-feature deviation when comparing a draft. Never return a human or AI authorship verdict. Do not infer biography, opinions, or personality facts from the corpus. Do not fabricate voice from one example.
