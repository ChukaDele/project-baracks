# Direct-response writing

Clarify the real offer, fit, evidence, objections, terms, and next action. Reject fake scarcity, fake urgency, fabricated proof, confirm-shaming, hidden terms, deceptive cancellation, and manipulative dark patterns. Do not leak conversion templates into academic, technical, strategic, or ordinary explanatory work.
