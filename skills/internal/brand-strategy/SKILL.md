# Brand Strategy

Determine the audience problem, differentiated truthful promise, message hierarchy, credible proof, objections, and desired understanding. Do not default to hype or direct-response formulas. Hand the approved strategic spine to Prose Craft. Never fabricate customer proof, market leadership, testimonials, or differentiation.
