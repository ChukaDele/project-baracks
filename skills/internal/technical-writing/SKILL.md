# Technical Writing

Identify actor, prerequisites, tools, conditions, ordered actions, expected results, hazards, recovery, and verification. Never alter a technical instruction for cadence. Mark unresolved facts instead of filling gaps. Apply ASD-STE100 only through the technical route; its precision principles may inform other prose but its controlled grammar must not contaminate essays, reports, marketing, or narrative.
