# Writing OS

The job is to preserve real thought and make it easier to understand. Do not manufacture originality through polished language.

## 1. Build the source packet before drafting

For every substantive piece, identify:
1. Reader, purpose, desired action, genre, length, and risk.
2. Source of truth: user notes, supplied files, approved prior writing, prior messages, conversations, research, or clearly marked general knowledge. Retrieve these before generating from a blank state.
3. The central claim or useful idea. For idea-led pieces, compress it to one Shiny Dime: the smallest viable idea the piece must orbit.
4. The evidence, example, experience, mechanism, or observation supporting it.
5. The tension: what is surprising, contested, contradictory, costly, or easy to misunderstand.
6. Point of view status: what the user clearly believes, what is inferred, and what remains unknown.
7. The strongest counterargument or limit.
8. Approved language: phrases, examples, metaphors, terms, and sentence patterns already used or explicitly approved.
9. Gaps that must remain neutral rather than invented.

A substantive draft may begin only when the packet contains a claim, support, point-of-view status, and evidence boundary. If the user's personal position is missing, write neutrally or expose the gap. Never invent a personal opinion, story, motive, lesson, or experience.

## 2. Keep four layers separate

**Thinking method:** use an authority for a decision method, such as research depth, argument testing, compression, or structure.

**Evidence:** use the actual source material. Authorities do not supply facts about the user's subject.

**Voice:** derive from an approved corpus. Do not blend famous writers into a synthetic house voice.

**Page architecture:** choose the arrangement that best serves the idea. Layout is allowed to vary by genre and argument.

Read `references/authority-map.md` before applying an authority. Each authority owns a narrow decision type and has an explicit stopping point.

For substantive idea-led work, consult `references/process-evidence.md` for the generation, research, distillation, voice-learning, and editing sequence.

For practical business, educational, framework, or problem-solving nonfiction, consult `references/alex-hormozi.md` when utility, scope, framework validity, simplification, or reader transformation is central.

For narrative, personal, persuasive, descriptive, speech, or other style-sensitive work, also consult `references/writing-examples-corpus.md`. Use it to test whether form matches meaning and to avoid turning good defaults into universal bans.

## 3. Prefer retrieval over invention

The model should first retrieve, select, arrange, compress, compare, and recombine approved thinking. Net-new claims require evidence or clear labeling as analysis. For personal or thought-leadership writing, user-supplied language and prior approved writing outrank generic fluency.

A sentence that could be pasted unchanged into thousands of pieces on the same topic is probably commodity language. Keep it only when it is necessary connective tissue. The memorable parts should come from a specific claim, example, mechanism, distinction, observation, or consequence.

## 4. Draft and edit

Route to the correct specialist, then invoke Prose Craft. Research and trace material claims when the genre requires it. Structure around the argument rather than a canned template.

Run voice matching only from an approved corpus. Writing samples outrank generic style rules unless meaning, evidence, safety, or the brief conflicts.

Run every gate in the route receipt. Lint and Natural Writing QA detect before targeted edits. The evaluator returns findings only. High-stakes work requires a critic that did not draft the text.

Revise identified defects, then rerun affected checks. Verify claims and source meaning. Detector scores never override facts or determine quality.

Treat accepted edits through the existing learning lifecycle using GLOBAL, GENRE, BRAND, PERSONAL VOICE, PROJECT, or ONE-OFF classification. Do not generalize one-off edits.

## 5. Hard failure conditions

Reject or revise a draft when it:
- invents a viewpoint, anecdote, quotation, statistic, case, credential, or source;
- substitutes abstract language for a missing idea;
- sounds polished while the claim remains generic;
- uses complexity to hide weak reasoning;
- imitates an authority's surface manner instead of applying the authority's decision method;
- strengthens certainty beyond the evidence;
- removes a necessary qualification for rhythm;
- introduces generic motivational or corporate language that the source packet does not support.

Meaning, evidence, legal effect, citations, and technical instructions outrank style cleanup. Controlled technical rules apply only on technical routes. Direct-response tactics apply only when commercial action is required and must reject deception, fake urgency, fake scarcity, fabricated proof, confirm-shaming, hidden terms, and dark patterns.
