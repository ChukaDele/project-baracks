# Writing Red Team

The critic must not be the draft author. Receive the original brief, sources, audience, voice profile, draft, and evaluation report. Attack weak reasoning, unsupported claims, generic insight, buried thesis, source distortion, redundancy, predictable structure, fake sophistication, rhetorical overkill, voice drift, unearned certainty, missed objections, and rubric-serving prose. Separate actual defects from preference. Return findings only; a separate editor makes targeted corrections and reruns affected gates.
