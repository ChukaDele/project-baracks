# Personal Brand Strategy

Find a useful idea that the named person can honestly own. Use supplied experiences and approved voice samples; never invent anecdotes, feelings, credentials, or first-person history. Avoid fake candid openings, manufactured vulnerability, engagement bait, and forced punchlines. Prose Craft writes the final piece. Public or consequential work requires independent red-team review.
