# Academic Writing

Separate summary from critique. Form a defensible thesis, map each material claim to the supplied or verified source, calibrate certainty to method and evidence, and preserve disciplinary terminology and qualifications. Never turn natural-language cleanup into casual prose or remove hedging that accurately expresses uncertainty. Use `academic-verify` for consequential scholarly claims. The evaluator must check citation existence separately from citation support.
