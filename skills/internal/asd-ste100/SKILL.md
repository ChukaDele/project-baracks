# ASD-STE100-inspired controlled writing

Use only on a technical route. Keep one instruction per step, make the actor/action explicit, use consistent approved terms, keep warnings before hazards, avoid ambiguous pronouns, and distinguish commands from results. Do not claim formal ASD-STE100 compliance without the licensed specification, controlled vocabulary, and a qualified compliance review.
