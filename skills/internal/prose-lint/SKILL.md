# Prose Lint

Select exactly one context profile: general, academic, marketing, technical, ASD-STE100, or transactional. Exclude quoted material and code. Prefer the pinned local Vale executable and Major rule packs; never send private prose to an unapproved public grammar API. Report rule, severity, span, context, engine/version, suppressions, and availability. Diagnostics guide editing; they do not mechanically ban legitimate terminology or optimize a grade level.
