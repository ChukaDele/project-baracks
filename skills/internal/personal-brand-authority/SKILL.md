# Personal brand authority

## Step 0: Decide the split

Answer first: which brand carries the trust, the person or the company?

- Person-led is faster to trust and cheaper to start. It does not sell without you.
- Company-led is slower and scales. It also survives a sale.

Default for a solo consultant: person-led front end, company-led delivery and contracts. Write down which assets belong to which brand. Do not blur them by accident.

## Step 1: Breakthrough idea (Clark)

Find the one idea you will be known for. Test it:

- Is it a claim, not a topic? "Recruitment firms should sell demand, not candidates" is a claim. "Recruitment marketing" is a topic.
- Could a competent peer disagree with it? If not, it is not an idea.
- Can you defend it with your own evidence?

## Step 2: Publish (Clark, Priestley)

- Choose one home channel and one format. Not five.
- Set a volume commitment you can hold for 90 days.
- Build a content spine: five themes that all support the breakthrough idea.
- Convert delivered work into published proof: teardowns, results, and process artefacts.

## Step 3: Proof assets (Priestley)

Priestley's five: Pitch, Publish, Product, Profile, Partnership.

- **Pitch:** one clear offer, priced, with the buying decision made easy.
- **Publish:** an owned asset that does the explaining — guide, framework, or short book.
- **Product:** a productised version of the service with a name and a fixed scope.
- **Profile:** third-party credibility — podcasts, guest posts, client names, awards.
- **Partnership:** a channel other people own that sends you buyers.

## Step 4: Reality check

Publishing is not selling. Set an outbound and pipeline target alongside the content target. Report both.

If revenue is the current constraint, cap authority work at a small share of the week and put the rest into direct selling. Say this to the user if it applies.

## Output format

1. Brand split decision, with an asset list per brand
2. Breakthrough idea, in one claim
3. Five content themes
4. 90-day publishing plan with volume
5. Priestley five-asset status and gaps
6. The metric that decides if this is working

## Limits

- Clark and Priestley teach visibility. Visibility is not demand.
- A personal brand does not fix an unclear offer. Use `brand-strategy` first.
