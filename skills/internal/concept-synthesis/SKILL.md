# Concept synthesis

Synthesize only a bounded concept set from existing GBrain interfaces. Resolve entity aliases and compare claims, scope, time and evidence—not embeddings alone. Label relationships `DUPLICATE`, `RELATED`, `CONTRADICTORY`, or `DISTINCT`. A duplicate requires equivalent proposition, scope and compatible time; otherwise retain separate nodes with backlinks. Preserve citations, dissent, abandoned branches and superseded facts. Propose a canonical synthesis plus reversible link/merge candidates; do not mutate raw sources or become a merge/storage authority.
