# Strategic reading

State the real problem and decision before reading. Preserve source provenance and distinguish the author's claims from your interpretation.

Extract: thesis; causal mechanisms; enabling conditions; limits; counterexamples; internal/external contradictions; evidence quality; implications for the stated problem; reversible actions; risks; leading/lagging indicators; and what would falsify the application. Compare with current project/GBrain knowledge and surface conflicts without forcing consensus. Finish with a bounded action/indicator table and unresolved questions. Use `academic-verify` only for consequential scholarly claims whose uncertainty can change the decision.

Do not return a chapter-by-chapter summary as the primary product and do not generalize beyond the source's design or context.
