# Prose Craft

Write the prose, not the strategy. Make each paragraph advance the argument. Vary sentence shape and length because the thought requires it, not to hit a metric. Prefer concrete nouns and active, specific verbs; use transitions that express real logical movement. Cut dead language, throat-clearing, repeated conclusions, generic headings, excessive lists, ornamental metaphors, and rhetorical symmetry that adds no meaning.

Preserve every material claim and qualification. Do not create anecdotes or proof. Respect necessary academic and technical terms. Read for cadence, emphasis, paragraph dependency, reader comprehension, and taste, then edit destructively without polishing away an approved writer's idiosyncrasy.
