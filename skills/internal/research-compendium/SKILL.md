# Research compendium

Create a bounded derived asset through existing GBrain interfaces with: question; scope/exclusions; source index and retrieval dates; claim-to-source map; findings; contradictions; uncertainty; gaps; synthesis; implications/actions; freshness policy; and project/entity links. Distinguish source claims, user conclusions and Major synthesis. Store citations, short necessary excerpts and derived knowledge—not copyrighted full text. Preserve superseded editions and provenance; update findings without rewriting underlying sources.
