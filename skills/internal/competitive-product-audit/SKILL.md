# Competitive Product Audit

- Select 3–5 closest/strongest products plus useful adjacent analogues.
- Prefer direct product use and official documentation.
- Study the critical workflow, not marketing claims.
- Capture hierarchy, defaults/progressive disclosure, states/recovery, trust/auditability, collaboration/ownership, reporting/decision support and speed for frequent users.
- Record what to adopt, adapt and avoid.
- Stop once the MVP flow and major design decisions are clear.
